** FLEX Equipment Finance (FLEX EF)

FLEX Equipment Finance is a SaaS platform and marketplace for equipment financing (B2B). FLEX facilitates equipment finance loan origination, servicing, and non-performing contract resolutions.

*** Key Features

**** Portals for Originators, Lenders, Borrowers, and Collectors

**** Integrations with:

***** DocuSign

***** PayNet

***** EquiFax

***** LexisNexis

***** Google Docs

**** Loan Underwriting Features

***** Credit report combined analsyis

***** Tax return and bank statement attachment detection

***** FLEX AI assistant to help with credit analysis

**** Loan Agreement Features

***** Flexible loan structuring including contact payments, skip payments, irregular payments, due date changes, etc.

***** DocuSign electronic signing workflows without leaving FLEX

***** ACH AutoPay account onboarding

**** Loan Servicing Features

***** AutoPay (ACH) account management

***** Alerts for failed payments

***** FLEX AI Assistent to help troubleshoot problem accounts

***** Notices (e.g. notice of default) mailing (both physical and email) with read receipts, tracking, and alerts when notice deadlines pass unresolved

***** Custom portfolio reports

***** Equipment for resale management

***** Deficiency tracking and management module
